## Where can information leak into a clinical prediction model?

Follow a patient through a small cohort study. Compare what happens when repeated samples cross the train–test split, when feature selection happens before cross-validation, and when the model is evaluated at a second hospital.

## What would it take to rerun this figure a year from now?

Trace a figure back to its inputs, parameters and software versions. Use a Nextflow and Quarto example to show how a colleague could reproduce it and investigate any differences.

## How does ancestry change along a chromosome?

Use simulated allele frequencies to show what a genome-wide average can hide and how TreeSwirl uses information from neighboring markers.
